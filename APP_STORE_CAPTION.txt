DESCRIPTION:
Wealth Forge is an addictive strategy idle game where you build your financial empire from nothing. Inspired by Universal Paperclips, this multi-phase wealth simulator lets you make real strategic decisions as you climb from broke to billionaire.

START WITH NOTHING
Tap to hustle. Every tap earns cash. Build your streak for massive multipliers. This is where legends begin.

CHOOSE YOUR PATH
Pick your career: Tech, Finance, Creator, or Trades. Each path offers unique bonuses, promotions, and meeting opportunities. Work your way from Junior Developer to CEO, from Analyst to Hedge Fund Legend.

INVEST STRATEGICALLY
Grow your wealth through smart investments. Start with savings accounts and index funds, then graduate to stocks, crypto, real estate, and eventually venture capital and private equity. Balance risk and reward.

SEIZE OPPORTUNITIES
Random opportunity cards appear with calculated risks. Take the hot stock tip? Accept the acquisition offer? The choice is yours - but choose wisely.

UNLOCK NEW PHASES
Progress through four distinct phases, each revealing new mechanics:
- Phase 1: HUSTLE - Tap, upgrade, and build your foundation
- Phase 2: CAREER & LEVERAGE - Choose your path, get promoted, use debt strategically  
- Phase 3: PORTFOLIO ENGINE - Launch products, diversify investments, automate income
- Phase 4: LEGACY & SCALE - Build your empire, influence the world, meet global leaders

CLIMB THE STATUS LADDER
Earn status points through promotions and achievements. Unlock meetings with project managers, then directors, then CEOs. Eventually, Tim Cook wants your advice. Elon Musk wants to collaborate. The President wants to meet you.

LAUNCH YOUR PRODUCT
In Phase 3, choose a product to bring to market. Mobile app? SaaS platform? Hardware startup? Pick your budget, roll the dice, and see if you've built the next unicorn.

NO ADS. NO GRINDING. PURE STRATEGY.
This is a premium game with no in-app purchases. Pay once, play forever. No paywalls, no timers, no manipulation - just strategic depth and satisfying progression.
