APP NAME: "Life of Wealth"

SUBTITLE: "Money is just the beginning."

TAGLINE: "What does a wealthy life actually look like? You decide."

PRICE: $2.99

DESCRIPTION:
Life of Wealth is an addictive life simulation strategy game where every decision shapes not just your bank account, but your entire life. Build financial empires, but ask yourself: at what cost?

FIVE DIMENSIONS OF WEALTH
True wealth isn't just money. Track and balance five dimensions of life:
- Financial (Cash, investments, net worth)
- Relationships (Family, friends, network depth)
- Experiences (Travel, adventures, memories)
- Health (Stress levels, work-life balance)
- Legacy (Impact, philanthropy, what you leave behind)

MAKE MEANINGFUL CHOICES
Every decision has real weight:
"Work overtime for a promotion?" → +Financial, -Health, -Relationships
"Take a family vacation?" → +Relationships, +Experiences, -Financial
"Grind through your 20s?" → +Financial, but what did you miss?

BUILD A FAMILY
Date, marry, and raise children. Each family member has their own personality:
- Supportive partners boost relationships
- Ambitious partners bring dual income
- Children need your time and attention
- Family events test your priorities

CHOOSE YOUR FACTION
Align with different networks to unlock unique opportunities:
- Corporate: Stability, hierarchy, steady growth
- Startup/Tech: High risk, high reward
- Old Money: Connections and status
- Creator: Audience-powered volatility

MULTIPLE ENDINGS
Your life's choices determine your legacy:
- The Tycoon: Billions, but died alone
- Balanced Life: Not the richest, but fulfilled
- Legacy Builder: Changed lives, built something lasting
- Family First: Rich in love, surrounded by generations
- Adventure Seeker: Passport full, stories legendary
- And more...

PRESTIGE SYSTEM
When your life ends, start again with the wisdom you gained. Different endings unlock different bonuses for your next life. How many lives will you live? What will you learn?

DEEP STRATEGIC GAMEPLAY
- Energy system forces prioritization
- Career paths with 8 promotion levels each
- 14+ investment types from savings to venture capital
- Random opportunities with calculated risks
- Products to launch and scale
- VIP contacts from managers to world leaders

NO ADS. NO GRINDING. PURE MEANING.
Premium game with no in-app purchases. Pay once, play forever. No paywalls, no timers - just meaningful choices and philosophical depth.
